Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. AI credits are one-time purchases. You are never billed again unless you choose to buy another package.

Do credits expire?

No. Purchased credits stay in your account until you use them.

What if the AI fails?

Credits are reserved when you send a request and only deducted after the change is successfully applied to your site. If anything fails, the reserved credits are returned automatically.

Can I use the builder without buying credits?

Yes. Templates, click-to-edit text and images, colors, fonts, basic animations, uploads and preview are all free.
