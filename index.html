System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • NVIDIA graphics card recommended for fast 4K (GTX 1060 or newer). Works without one, just slower.
  • ~1 GB of free disk space for the app, plus space for your 4K files
  • Internet connection for the one-time install and for downloading from links

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no watermark, no account.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

Yes – Windows shows this for any new script downloaded from the internet. Click More info → Run anyway.

Can AI make a blurry 240p video look like real 4K?

It helps a lot – it removes noise and compression and sharpens edges – but it can't invent details that were never filmed. The better the source, the better the result.

Where are my files saved?

You download each result from the app, and a copy is also kept in the outputs folder inside the 4K Studio folder.

Can I download any video?

Only download videos you own or have permission to use, and respect each site's terms of service and copyright.
