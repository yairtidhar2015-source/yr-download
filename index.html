FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no watermark, no subscription.

Why is the first run slow?

The AI model downloads once (5 MB for 4K upscaling, about 200 MB for text removal). After that your browser remembers it. Chrome or Edge on a computer with a graphics card is fastest.

Are my images uploaded?

No. All processing happens in your browser on your own device.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC" when installing the app

Windows shows this for any new script from the internet. Click More info → Run anyway.

Can AI turn a tiny blurry picture into a perfect 4K photo?

It helps a lot – sharper edges, less noise and compression – but it can't invent details that were never captured.

Can I download any video with the PC app?

Only download videos you own or have permission to use, and respect each site's terms and copyright.
